Aura Clock: The Beautifully Minimal Bedside or Desktop Companion

Aura Clock is a stunningly simple and minimal digital clock designed to transform your device into an elegant, full-screen timepiece. Perfect for nightstands, desks, or any dedicated display, Aura Clock puts beautiful, legible time first.



✨ Key Features Designed for Sleep and Focus:



Minimalist Full-Screen Display: Get rid of distractions with a clean, borderless time display.



Customizable Aura Glow: Activate the unique "Aura Effect" that applies a subtle, soft glow around the time digits, creating a soothing ambient light tailored to your chosen color.



Adjustable Brightness: Precisely control the screen brightness to prevent disruptive light in a dark room. Dial it down to a faint whisper or boost it for daytime visibility.



Color Themes: Choose from five easy for eyes curated color palettes (Lime, Blue, Yellow, Red, Light Grey) to match your mood or decor.



Keep Screen Awake Toggle: Use the dedicated setting to prevent your screen from sleeping, ensuring the time is always visible (ideal for bedside use).



🔔 Utility \& Alerts:



Chime Alert (Foreground Only): Set a daily chime at a specific time (e.g., 8:00 AM) to greet the start of your day with a simple, pleasant melody. The chime only triggers when the app is actively open on your screen.



Countdown Timer: Use the built-in timer for focus sessions, cooking, or short reminders. The timer triggers the selected melody upon completion.



Multiple Melodies: Select from a variety of pleasant short tones and sounds (Gong, Woosh, Clock, Bell, Ancient, Flute, Tick-tock, Tunnel) for your chimes and timers.



⚙️ Display Options:



12/24 Hour Format: Easily switch between standard time display formats.



Show Seconds: Toggle the display of seconds for precise timing or hide them for an even cleaner look.



Aura Clock is built to be a beautiful, distraction-free utility. All settings and preferences are saved locally on your device, ensuring a fast, private, and consistent experience every time you open the app. Download Aura Clock today and make the time beautiful!
